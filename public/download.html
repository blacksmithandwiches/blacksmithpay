Android

Download the Android app (.apk, 14.1 MB)

  1. Tap the button and let the file download.
  2. Open the file. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser — this is the standard step for apps downloaded outside Google Play.
  3. Open BlacksmithPay and try the test-money wallet.

iPhone & iPad

Apple does not allow direct app-file downloads. Use the installable web version instead: open the BlacksmithPay demo in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen — it installs and behaves like an app.

On the web

No install needed: open BlacksmithPay in your browser.

About the crypto side

The app's crypto features connect straight to the Ethereum network — no single company's wallet is required. Bring any Ethereum wallet you already have (for example MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet or Brave Wallet), or use the app's built-in demo wallet: it creates an account right in your browser, nothing to install. Your wallet holds your crypto and asks your permission before anything moves; BlacksmithPay never asks for a recovery phrase or a private key. The app explains all of this on its home page, in the section "Crypto connects to Ethereum — your choice of wallet". Test network only (Sepolia) — test ETH has no value, and the demo wallet is for test funds only: never send real funds to it.
