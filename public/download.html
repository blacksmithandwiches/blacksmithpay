Download BlacksmithPay

Send money like a message — fiat and crypto in one wallet. This is the public prototype build.

TEST MONEY ONLY. This build moves no real money. Balances are a mock ledger and crypto runs on the Sepolia test network. Do not send real funds to any address in this app.

Android

Download the Android app (.apk, 3.6 MB)

  1. Tap the button and let the file download.
  2. Open the file. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser — this is the standard step for apps downloaded outside Google Play.
  3. Open BlacksmithPay and try the test-money wallet.

iPhone & iPad

Apple does not allow direct app-file downloads. Use the installable web version instead: open the BlacksmithPay demo in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen — it installs and behaves like an app.

On the web

No install needed: open BlacksmithPay in your browser.
